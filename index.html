How to use this book

  1. Do one chapter a day (15–20 minutes). Start with the Essential ones.
  2. Read the rule, then read the Tamil box to connect it with what you know.
  3. Say every example aloud – speaking is a muscle, reading alone won't build it.
  4. Do the “Speak it” tasks out loud, then take the quiz. Wrong? Read why and try again.
  5. Tap “Mark chapter done” and come back tomorrow.
